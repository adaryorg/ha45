# Turkey And Bean Chili

This makes several proper meals, freezes well, and tastes even better the next day. In other words, it rewards planning without requiring much of it.

## Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :-- | --: |
| Lean ground turkey | 600 g |
| Kidney beans, drained | 1 can / about 240 g |
| Black beans, drained | 1 can / about 240 g |
| Chopped tomatoes | 2 × 400 g cans |
| Onion, diced | 1 large |
| Red bell pepper, diced | 1 large |
| Olive oil | 1 tablespoon |
| Tomato paste | 2 tablespoons |
| Ground cumin | 2 teaspoons |
| Smoked paprika | 2 teaspoons |
| Chili powder | 1–2 teaspoons |
| Salt and pepper | To taste |

## Preparation

1. Heat the oil in a large pot and soften the onion and pepper for 5 minutes.
2. Add the turkey and cook until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon.
3. Stir in the tomato paste and spices, then add the tomatoes and beans.
4. Simmer uncovered for 25–35 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add a little water if it becomes too thick.
5. Taste, adjust the seasoning, and serve. Rice, yogurt, avocado, or none of the above are all legitimate choices.
