# Tofu Peanut Noodle Bowl

Proof that a meal does not require animal protein to be satisfying—or require anyone to make a lifestyle announcement before eating it.

## Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :-- | --: |
| Extra-firm tofu, pressed | 400 g |
| Dry whole-wheat or rice noodles | 140 g |
| Red bell pepper, sliced | 1 |
| Carrot, shredded | 1 large |
| Edamame, shelled | 150 g |
| Peanut butter | 30 g |
| Low-sodium soy sauce | 2 tablespoons |
| Lime juice | 2 tablespoons |
| Maple syrup | 2 teaspoons |
| Sesame oil | 1 teaspoon |
| Fresh ginger, grated | 1 teaspoon |
| Chili flakes | To taste |

## Preparation

1. Cube the tofu and cook it in a non-stick pan until browned on several sides.
2. Cook the noodles according to the package instructions. Add the edamame for the final few minutes, then drain.
3. Mix the peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, maple syrup, sesame oil, ginger, chili, and enough warm water to make a smooth sauce.
4. Combine the noodles, edamame, tofu, pepper, carrot, and sauce.
