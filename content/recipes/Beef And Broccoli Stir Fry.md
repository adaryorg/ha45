# Beef And Broccoli Stir Fry

Fast enough for a weekday and considerably better than ordering something that arrives mostly composed of sauce.

## Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :-- | --: |
| Lean beef strips | 300 g |
| Cooked brown or white rice | 300 g |
| Broccoli florets | 300 g |
| Carrot, thinly sliced | 1 medium |
| Neutral cooking oil | 2 teaspoons |
| Low-sodium soy sauce | 2 tablespoons |
| Rice vinegar | 1 tablespoon |
| Honey | 2 teaspoons |
| Fresh ginger, grated | 2 teaspoons |
| Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| Cornstarch | 1 teaspoon |

## Preparation

1. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar, honey, ginger, garlic, cornstarch, and two tablespoons of water.
2. Heat one teaspoon of oil in a very hot pan. Cook the beef quickly, then remove it.
3. Add the remaining oil, broccoli, carrot, and a splash of water. Stir-fry until just tender.
4. Return the beef, pour in the sauce, and cook for another minute until glossy and thickened.
5. Divide the rice between two bowls and add the stir-fry.
