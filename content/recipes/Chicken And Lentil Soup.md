# Chicken And Lentil Soup

This is soup that understands it is supposed to be a meal, not warm flavored water served before one.

## Ingredients

Makes 4 servings.

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :-- | --: |
| Boneless chicken breast or thighs | 500 g |
| Dry green or brown lentils | 200 g |
| Carrots, diced | 2 medium |
| Celery stalks, diced | 2 |
| Onion, diced | 1 large |
| Chopped tomatoes | 1 × 400 g can |
| Chicken stock | 1.25 litres |
| Olive oil | 1 tablespoon |
| Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| Ground cumin | 1 teaspoon |
| Dried thyme | 1 teaspoon |
| Salt and pepper | To taste |

## Preparation

1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Cook the onion, carrot, and celery for 6–8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, cumin, and thyme, followed by the lentils, tomatoes, stock, and whole chicken pieces.
3. Simmer gently for 25–30 minutes, until the lentils and chicken are cooked.
4. Remove the chicken, shred it, and return it to the pot. Season to taste.
