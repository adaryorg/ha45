# Salmon And Sweet Potato Tray Bake

One tray, very little ceremony, and a meal that looks considerably more organized than the person who made it.

## Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :-- | --: |
| Salmon fillets | 2 × 150 g |
| Sweet potato, cubed | 400 g |
| Broccoli florets | 250 g |
| Olive oil | 1 tablespoon |
| Dijon mustard | 1 tablespoon |
| Lemon | 1 |
| Garlic, minced | 2 cloves |
| Smoked paprika | 1/2 teaspoon |
| Salt and pepper | To taste |

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 210°C.
2. Toss the sweet potato with half the oil, paprika, salt, and pepper. Roast for 20 minutes.
3. Add the broccoli to the tray. Place the salmon alongside it.
4. Mix the remaining oil with the mustard, garlic, lemon zest, and a squeeze of lemon juice. Brush it over the salmon.
5. Roast for another 12–15 minutes, until the salmon is cooked to your liking and the vegetables are tender.
