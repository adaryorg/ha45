# Egg And Cottage Cheese Breakfast Wrap

A breakfast with enough substance to survive contact with the rest of the morning.

## Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :-- | --: |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Cottage cheese | 100 g |
| Whole-wheat tortilla | 1 large |
| Avocado | 50 g |
| Baby spinach | 1 large handful |
| Tomato, diced | 1 small |
| Olive oil or cooking spray | 1 teaspoon |
| Hot sauce, optional | To taste |
| Salt and pepper | To taste |

## Preparation

1. Beat the eggs with the cottage cheese, salt, and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a pan, wilt the spinach, and add the egg mixture. Cook gently until just set.
3. Warm the tortilla, then add the eggs, tomato, avocado, and hot sauce.
4. Fold firmly enough that breakfast does not fall into your lap.
