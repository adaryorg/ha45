# Recipes

<p class="page-kicker">Food For Real Life</p>

These are recipes I actually prepare and eat—not meals assembled by a food stylist, photographed under studio lighting, and then quietly abandoned because nobody wants to wash fourteen pans on a Tuesday night.

Most of them are straightforward, reasonably nutritious, and made from ingredients that can be found without joining a waiting list. Some are high in protein, some are useful before or after training, and some are simply good food. I am not trying to turn every meal into an optimization problem. It should taste good, fit into real life, and not require the afternoon off.

Quantities and nutritional values are estimates. Brands, substitutions, serving sizes, and the enthusiastic application of maple syrup can all change the numbers.
