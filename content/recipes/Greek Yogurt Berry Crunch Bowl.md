# Greek Yogurt Berry Crunch Bowl

For mornings when cooking feels excessively ambitious but eating directly from a cereal box feels like surrender.

## Ingredients

Makes 1 serving.

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :-- | --: |
| Greek yogurt, 2–5% fat | 250 g |
| Mixed berries | 150 g |
| Rolled oats | 35 g |
| Walnuts, roughly chopped | 15 g |
| Chia seeds | 10 g |
| Maple syrup or honey | 10 g |
| Cinnamon | To taste |

## Preparation

1. Put the yogurt in a bowl.
2. Add the berries, oats, walnuts, chia seeds, maple syrup, and cinnamon.
3. Eat immediately for maximum crunch, or leave it for 10–15 minutes if you prefer the oats slightly softened.
