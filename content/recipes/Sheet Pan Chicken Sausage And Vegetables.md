# Sheet Pan Chicken Sausage And Vegetables

Chop things, put them on a tray, and let the oven do the part that usually requires competence.

## Ingredients

Makes 2 servings.

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :-- | --: |
| Good-quality chicken sausages | 300 g |
| Baby potatoes, halved | 350 g |
| Red bell pepper, chopped | 1 |
| Courgette, chopped | 1 medium |
| Red onion, cut into wedges | 1 small |
| Olive oil | 2 teaspoons |
| Dried oregano | 1 teaspoon |
| Smoked paprika | 1 teaspoon |
| Dijon mustard | 1 tablespoon |
| Salt and pepper | To taste |

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 210°C.
2. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and roast for 15 minutes.
3. Add the sausages, pepper, courgette, onion, remaining oil, oregano, paprika, salt, and pepper.
4. Roast for another 20–25 minutes, turning once, until the sausages are fully cooked and the vegetables are browned.
5. Serve with the mustard.
